Equity valuation · Financial modelingFeb 2025

Costco (COST) Equity Research & Valuation

Question

What growth is the market pricing into Costco, and does the business support it?

Model

FY2020–2024 statements rebuilt from 10-Ks into a driver-based, fully linked three-statement forecast to FY2029; DCF with bear-to-bull range; seven trading comps; reverse DCF.

Recommendation

The share price only works if Costco grows about 7% a year forever. My base case doesn't support that, so the valuation risk is on the downside. Written up as an investment memo and deck.

Credit risk · Model validationApr 2024

Consumer Credit PD Model Development & Validation

Question

Which accounts will default, what will it cost, and where should the approval cutoff sit?

Model

Five probability-of-default models compared; XGBoost picked on validation data and calibrated to feed expected loss (PD × LGD × EAD) under base and severe stress. Self-validated following SR 11-7.

Recommendation

A 45% PD cutoff cuts the approved book's default rate by a third while still approving 85% of applicants. Recalibrate before use: a 0.91 calibration slope left expected loss about 2.7% too optimistic.

0.778ROC-AUC on an untouched test set (0.556 Gini)
22.1→14.9%approved-book default rate at the 45% cutoff
+157%expected loss under the severe stress scenario

Market risk · Portfolio analytics2026

Multi-Asset Portfolio Risk & Stress-Testing Framework

Question

Does portfolio construction change risk more than return, and which VaR model holds up in a crisis?

Model

Equal-weight, minimum-variance and 8% volatility-target portfolios backtested against a 60/40 over 19 years, net of costs. Historical, parametric and Monte Carlo VaR and expected shortfall, tested with Kupiec, Christoffersen and Basel traffic-light tests, plus 6 historical crises and factor and correlation stresses.

Recommendation

Choose construction by risk budget, since returns barely differ. Don't rely on normal VaR at 99%: it failed for every portfolio and breaches clustered.

0.82–0.84Sharpe for all three methods, vs 0.66 for the 60/40

Fraud · Cost-benefit analysisOct 2024

Fraud Detection Model & Risk-Threshold Optimization

Question

Where should the alert threshold sit to catch the most fraud with the review team we have?

Model

Fraud model on point-in-time SQL features with a time-based split, benchmarked against a rules baseline. Threshold set by weighing missed-fraud losses against review cost, with ±50% sensitivity analysis and a simulation of the full alert workflow.

Recommendation

Move to the model with the cost-based threshold: fewer alerts, more fraud caught, and about $190K lower simulated cost over 129 days (95% CI $150K–$231K).
